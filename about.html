About Labistry

Practical scientific tools for everyday laboratory calculations.

What is Labistry?

Labistry is a collection of practical scientific calculators and laboratory resources designed to make common calculations faster, clearer and easier to reproduce.


The platform focuses on calculations frequently encountered in laboratory work, particularly in molecular biology, chemistry and life sciences.

Our goal

🧪 Practical tools

Provide simple calculators that can be used directly during routine laboratory work.

📚 Clear explanations

Complement calculations with concise explanations of the underlying scientific concepts.

🔬 Laboratory focused

Focus on calculations and workflows that are relevant to real laboratory applications.

🌐 Accessible

Make useful scientific tools easily accessible without requiring specialized software.

Who is Labistry for?

Labistry is intended for researchers, laboratory technicians, students and scientists who need quick access to common laboratory calculations.


The tools are designed to support laboratory work, while users remain responsible for checking calculations, experimental conditions and manufacturer-specific recommendations.
