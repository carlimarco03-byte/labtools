About Labistry

Practical scientific tools designed to make laboratory calculations faster, clearer and more accessible.

What is Labistry?

Labistry is a collection of free scientific calculators and laboratory resources designed for researchers, students and scientists.


The platform focuses on practical calculations commonly encountered in chemistry, molecular biology, biotechnology and life sciences.

Why Labistry?

⚡ Fast

Perform common laboratory calculations without manually setting up formulas every time.

🧪 Practical

Tools are designed around real laboratory workflows and commonly used experimental calculations.

🌐 Accessible

Labistry is designed to provide useful scientific tools directly from the browser, without requiring additional software.

What can you find on Labistry?

🧪 Solution Preparation

Dilution, molarity, concentration conversion and other solution-preparation calculations.

🧬 Molecular Biology

PCR, qPCR, RT-PCR and DNA-related calculations and tools.

📊 Statistics

Scientific statistical tools designed for common research workflows.

⚗ Chemistry

Practical chemical calculations and concentration-related tools.

Important note

Labistry calculators are intended as educational and practical calculation aids. Users should independently verify calculations, units, concentrations and experimental conditions before using the results in laboratory work.
