Percentage Solutions

Learn how to calculate and prepare laboratory solutions expressed as percentage weight/volume, volume/volume and weight/weight.

What is a percentage solution?

A percentage solution describes the amount of solute relative to a defined amount of solution or solvent.


In laboratory practice, three common notation systems are used:

% w/v

Weight per volume.

Grams of solute per 100 mL of final solution.

% v/v

Volume per volume.

Milliliters of solute per 100 mL of final solution.

% w/w

Weight per weight.

Grams of solute per 100 g of final solution.

% w/v — Weight per volume

A % w/v solution expresses the mass of solute in grams contained in 100 mL of final solution.

% w/v = grams of solute / 100 mL of solution

Example: 5% w/v NaCl

A 5% w/v NaCl solution contains:

5 g NaCl / 100 mL solution

To prepare 500 mL:

5 × 500 / 100 = 25 g

Therefore, weigh 25 g of NaCl and dissolve it in an appropriate amount of solvent. Then bring the final solution volume to 500 mL.

% v/v — Volume per volume

A % v/v solution expresses the volume of liquid solute contained in 100 mL of final solution.

% v/v = mL of solute / 100 mL of solution

Example: 70% v/v ethanol

A 70% v/v ethanol solution contains:

70 mL ethanol / 100 mL solution

To prepare 250 mL:

70 × 250 / 100 = 175 mL

Therefore, measure 175 mL of ethanol and add appropriate solvent until the final volume is 250 mL.

% w/w — Weight per weight

A % w/w solution expresses the mass of solute relative to the total mass of the final solution.

% w/w = grams of solute / grams of solution × 100

Example: 10% w/w solution

A 10% w/w solution contains 10 g of solute for every 100 g of final solution.


To prepare 500 g of solution:

10 × 500 / 100 = 50 g

Therefore, 50 g of solute are required. The remaining 450 g correspond to the other components of the final solution.

Comparing the three formats

% w/v

Mass of solute
per 100 mL solution.

Common for aqueous laboratory solutions and biological reagents.

% v/v

Volume of solute
per 100 mL solution.

Common for liquid reagents such as ethanol solutions.

% w/w

Mass of solute
per 100 g solution.

Useful when working with mixtures defined by mass.

Important considerations

The denominator always refers to the final solution or mixture, not necessarily the amount of solvent initially added.


For example, when preparing a 5% w/v solution, 5 g of solute should be dissolved and the final volume adjusted to 100 mL. It does not mean adding 5 g of solute to exactly 100 mL of solvent.


Always check the intended definition of percentage concentration in the experimental protocol before preparing a solution.
