About statistical calculations

Descriptive statistics provide a concise summary of a dataset. They can help researchers understand the central tendency and variability of experimental measurements.


Central tendency

Mean and median describe the typical or central value of a dataset.

Variability

Standard deviation and variance describe how widely observations are distributed around the mean.

Data range

Minimum, maximum and range provide a simple overview of the spread of the observed values.
