About percentage solutions

Percentage solutions express the amount of solute relative to a defined amount of final solution. For w/v solutions, the percentage represents grams of solute per 100 mL of solution. For v/v solutions, it represents millilitres of solute per 100 mL of solution. For w/w solutions, it represents grams of solute per 100 g of solution.
