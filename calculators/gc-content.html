Enter a DNA sequence to calculate GC content.

How it works

GC content is the percentage of guanine (G) and cytosine (C) bases in a DNA sequence. The GC content is calculated by dividing the total number of G and C bases by the total number of valid DNA bases and multiplying by 100.
