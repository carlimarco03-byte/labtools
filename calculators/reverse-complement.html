DNA Reverse Complement Calculator

Generate the complementary, reverse and reverse complementary sequences of a DNA strand.

Enter a DNA sequence to calculate its reverse complement.

How it works

The complementary DNA strand is generated by replacing each nucleotide according to the base-pairing rules: A pairs with T, while G pairs with C. The reverse complement is obtained by reversing the complementary sequence.
