Methodological comparison

Labistry current: mid-ranks, U = min(U1,U2), uncorrected variance, no continuity correction.

Reference standard: mid-ranks, U = min(U1,U2), tie-corrected variance and continuity correction.

Exact reference: an independent enumeration of the exact U distribution is also calculated for small samples without ties.

The main purpose is to determine whether differences originate from the calculation of U itself or only from the inferential approximation used for z and p.
